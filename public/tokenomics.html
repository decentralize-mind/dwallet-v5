Deflationary Model

DWT implements a deflationary token model through an ongoing buyback-and-burn mechanism. Twenty percent (20%) of all platform transaction fees are allocated to purchasing DWT tokens from open market liquidity pools and permanently burning them by sending to the null address. Burn events are conducted monthly and are fully transparent, with all transactions verifiable on Etherscan and BaseScan. The burn rate is expected to increase as platform usage grows, creating deflationary pressure on the total circulating supply.
