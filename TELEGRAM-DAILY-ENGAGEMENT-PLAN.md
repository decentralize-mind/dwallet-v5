## 🚨 Community Management Tips

### **DO:**
✅ Respond within 2 hours  
✅ Be friendly and professional  
✅ Admit when you don't know  
✅ Escalate complex issues  
✅ Celebrate community wins  
✅ Share behind-the-scenes content  
✅ Ask for feedback regularly  
✅ Thank members for contributions  

### **DON'T:**
❌ Ignore questions  
❌ Delete negative feedback (unless spam)  
❌ Argue with members  
❌ Make promises you can't keep  
❌ Share sensitive information  
❌ Play favorites  
❌ Over-promote  
❌ Be inactive for days

## 📞 Emergency Response Plan

### **If Scam Appears:**
1. Delete immediately ⚡
2. Ban the user 🔨
3. Warn the community ⚠️
4. Pin warning message 📌
5. Review security settings 🔒

### **If FUD Spreads:**
1. Acknowledge concerns 🤝
2. Provide facts 📊
3. Stay professional 😊
4. Don't argue ❌
5. Follow up with updates 📢

### **If Technical Issue:**
1. Acknowledge quickly ⚡
2. Provide status updates 📊
3. Give ETA for fix ⏰
4. Thank for patience 🙏
5. Follow up when resolved ✅
