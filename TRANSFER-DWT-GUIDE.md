2. Click **"Contract"** tab
3. Click **"Write Contract"**

6. Fill in:
   - `to`: `0xE721B35b248BC5776963443DFC93E66BC17e3a5a`
   - `amount`: `5000000000000000000000` (that's 5000 with 18 decimals)
7. Click **"Write"**
8. Confirm the transaction in MetaMask
9. Wait for confirmation (~2-5 seconds on Base)

### Step 3: Verify the Transfer

1. Check your new wallet balance:
   https://basescan.org/address/0xE721B35b248BC5776963443DFC93E66BC17e3a5a

## ✅ OPTION 2: Use MetaMask Directly

### Step 1: Import Old Wallet to MetaMask

1. Open MetaMask
2. Click your account icon → **"Import Account"**
3. Select **"Private Key"**
4. Paste the private key of your old wallet (`0x830E...`)
5. Click **"Import"**

### Step 2: Add DWT Token to MetaMask

1. Make sure you're on **Base Mainnet** network
2. Click **"Import tokens"** at the bottom
3. Paste DWT contract address: `0x9ce235f8574bde67393884550F02135CE4fB8387`
4. MetaMask will auto-fill the symbol (DWT) and decimals (18)
5. Click **"Add Custom Token"**

### Step 3: Send DWT

1. Click on **DWT** token in your MetaMask
2. Click **"Send"**
3. Enter recipient address: `0xE721B35b248BC5776963443DFC93E66BC17e3a5a`
4. Enter amount: `5000`
5. Click **"Next"** → **"Confirm"**
6. Wait for transaction to complete

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Insufficient Balance" Error
- Check that your old wallet actually has 680,000 DWT on Base mainnet
- Verify on BaseScan: https://basescan.org/address/0x830E4DF895f967Ff2A29d1705DeB40CFc6d30b88

### "Insufficient Gas" Error
- You need ETH in your old wallet for transaction fees
- Buy a small amount of ETH on Base (~$1 worth is enough)
- Use https://onramp.coinbase.com or bridge from Ethereum

### Transaction Stuck/Pending
- Base transactions are usually instant (2-5 seconds)
- If stuck, check BaseScan for the transaction hash
- You may need to increase gas price slightly

### Wrong Network
- Make sure you're on **Base Mainnet** (Chain ID: 8453)
- NOT Sepolia, NOT Base Sepolia, NOT Ethereum mainnet
- DWT on testnets has NO value
