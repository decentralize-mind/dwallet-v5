## 📋 4 Main Actions

| Action | What It Does | Earn/Pay | Risk |
|--------|--------------|----------|------|
| **Supply** | Deposit crypto | Earn APY ✅ | Low |
| **Withdraw** | Take back deposits | - | Medium* |
| **Borrow** | Take loan | Pay APY ❌ | High |
| **Repay** | Pay back loan | - | Low |

*If you have borrows, watch health factor!

## ⚡ Quick Steps

### To SUPPLY (Earn Interest):
1. Click **"Lend"** tab
2. Select **"Supply"** mode
3. Choose asset
4. Enter amount (or MAX)
5. Click **"Supply"**
6. ✅ Done!

### To WITHDRAW:
1. Click **"Lend"** tab
2. Select **"Supply"** → **"Withdraw"**
3. Choose asset
4. Enter amount
5. Click **"Withdraw"**
6. ✅ Done!

### To BORROW:
1. Click **"Lend"** tab
2. Select **"Borrow"** mode
3. Choose asset
4. Enter amount
5. ⚠️ Check Health Factor!
6. Click **"Borrow"**
7. ✅ Done!

### To REPAY:
1. Click **"Lend"** tab
2. Select **"Borrow"** → **"Repay"**
3. Choose asset
4. Enter amount
5. Click **"Repay"**
6. ✅ Done!

## 🎯 Health Factor Guide

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Health Factor Status                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ > 2.0   ✅ SAFE (Good)               │
│ 1.5-2.0 ⚠️  WATCH (Be careful)       │
│ 1.0-1.5 🔴  DANGER (Very risky)      │
│ < 1.0   🚨  CRITICAL (Liquidation!)  │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘

✅ GOAL: Keep above 1.5 at all times!
```

## 💡 Best Practices

### ✅ DO:
- Supply stablecoins for highest APY (4-5%)
- Keep health factor > 1.5
- Monitor positions regularly
- Have ETH for gas fees
- Start with small amounts to learn

### ❌ DON'T:
- Max out borrowing limit
- Ignore health factor warnings
- Forget about interest payments
- Supply all funds (keep some for gas)
- Panic during market volatility

## ⚠️ Common Errors

| Error | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| "Insufficient Balance" | Not enough tokens | Add funds to wallet |
| "Health Factor Too Low" | Too much borrowed | Repay debt or add collateral |
| "Allowance" issue | Permission needed | Wait for approval tx |
| Transaction stuck | Low gas price | Wait or increase gas |

## 🎓 Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **APY** | Annual Percentage Yield (earnings rate) |
| **LTV** | Loan-to-Value (how much you can borrow) |
| **Health Factor** | Safety ratio (must be > 1.0) |
| **Collateral** | Assets you supply as security |
| **Liquidation** | Loss of collateral if HF < 1.0 |
| **Gas** | Transaction fee (paid in ETH) |
